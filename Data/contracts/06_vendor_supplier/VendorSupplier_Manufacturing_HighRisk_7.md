## 1. PRODUCTS AND SPECIFICATIONS

Supplier agrees to manufacture and supply precision machined automotive components ("Products") according to Buyer's specifications:

**Product Line:**
- Engine mounting brackets (Part #AMB-3344)
- Transmission housings (Part #TH-9821)
- Brake assembly components (Part #BAC-5567)
- Custom fasteners and hardware (Various part numbers)

**Specifications:** Per Buyer's technical drawings and quality standards (attached as Exhibit A).

**Quality Standards:** ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing. All Products must pass Buyer's quality inspections.

## 2. PRICING AND PAYMENT

**Pricing:** As specified in attached Price Schedule (Exhibit B).

**Minimum Order:** $50,000 per quarter

**Payment Terms:**
- Net 60 days from delivery and acceptance
- 2% discount if paid within 10 days
- Late payments: 1.5% monthly interest + right to suspend deliveries

**Price Adjustments:** Supplier may adjust prices annually with 90 days notice based on documented material cost changes. Increases capped at 8% annually.

## 3. TERM AND VOLUME

**Initial Term:** 3 years commencing February 1, 2025

**Estimated Annual Volume:** $800,000-1,200,000

**Exclusive Supplier:** Supplier is Buyer's exclusive source for Products during term. Buyer is not obligated to purchase exclusively from Supplier but will give Supplier right of first refusal.

**Renewal:** Automatically renews for 1-year terms unless either party provides 120 days written notice.

## 4. DELIVERY AND LOGISTICS

**Lead Time:** 6-8 weeks from purchase order

**Delivery:** FOB Supplier's facility. Buyer arranges and pays for shipping.

**Inspection:** Buyer has 10 business days to inspect and reject non-conforming Products.

**Late Delivery:** If Supplier is more than 14 days late, Buyer may source from alternate supplier and charge Supplier the price difference.

## 5. WARRANTIES

**Supplier Warrants:**
- Products manufactured to specifications and free from defects in materials and workmanship
- Products comply with all applicable laws and regulations
- Supplier has right to sell Products
- Products do not infringe third-party IP rights

**Warranty Period:** 24 months from delivery

**Remedy:** Repair or replacement at Supplier's option. Supplier pays return shipping for defective Products.

**DISCLAIMER:** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED, SUPPLIER DISCLAIMS ALL WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**Supplier's Liability Cap:** Total fees paid by Buyer in prior 12 months

**Excluded Damages:** Supplier not liable for indirect, consequential, incidental, special, or punitive damages, including lost profits, production delays, or business interruption.

**Product Liability:** Buyer shall indemnify Supplier for claims arising from Buyer's finished products incorporating Supplier's components, except for defects in Supplier's Products.

## 7. INDEMNIFICATION

**Supplier Indemnifies Buyer For:**
- Product defects causing injury or property damage
- Non-compliance with specifications
- IP infringement by Products
- Breach of warranties

**Buyer Indemnifies Supplier For:**
- Claims arising from Buyer's product designs
- Buyer's specifications or requirements
- Buyer's finished products (except Supplier defects)
- Buyer's use or misuse of Products

## 8. QUALITY CONTROL

**Certifications:** Supplier maintains ISO 9001:2015 certification throughout term.

**Inspections:** Buyer may inspect Supplier's facilities with 14 days notice, twice annually.

**Corrective Action:** Supplier must implement corrective actions for quality issues within 30 days or Buyer may terminate.

**First Article Inspection:** Supplier provides samples for approval before production runs.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**Buyer's IP:** All drawings, specifications, tooling designs, and technical information provided by Buyer remain Buyer's property.

**Tooling:** Buyer pays for custom tooling. Tooling remains Buyer's property and stored at Supplier's facility. Upon termination, Supplier ships tooling to Buyer at Buyer's expense.

**Improvements:** Any improvements to Products or manufacturing processes developed during engagement are jointly owned.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties protect confidential information for 3 years. Includes specifications, pricing, business terms, and proprietary processes.

## 11. TERMINATION

**Termination for Convenience:** Either party may terminate with 180 days written notice.

**Termination for Cause:**
- Material breach uncured within 30 days
- Bankruptcy or insolvency
- Loss of required certifications
- Consistent quality failures

**Wind-Down:** Upon termination, Buyer may place final orders for 6-month inventory. Supplier must fulfill orders in progress.

## 12. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither party liable for delays due to events beyond reasonable control (natural disasters, strikes, material shortages, government actions, pandemics). Party must notify within 5 days. If force majeure exceeds 90 days, either party may terminate without penalty.

## 13. COMPLIANCE

**Regulatory Compliance:** Supplier complies with all applicable laws including environmental, labor, and safety regulations.

**Conflict Minerals:** Supplier certifies Products are "DRC conflict-free" per Dodd-Frank Act.

**Export Controls:** Both parties comply with export control laws.

## 14. INSURANCE

Supplier maintains:
- Commercial General Liability: $2,000,000
- Product Liability: $5,000,000
- Workers Compensation: Statutory limits

Certificates provided annually.

## 15. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**Governing Law:** Michigan law

**Disputes:** Good-faith negotiation, then mediation, then litigation in Detroit, Michigan courts. Prevailing party recovers reasonable attorney fees.

## 16. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Entire Agreement:** Supersedes all prior agreements.
**Amendments:** Written, signed by both parties.
**Assignment:** Requires consent, except to successor entity.
**Purchase Orders:** Individual POs incorporate this Agreement's terms.
**Notices:** Via email and certified mail.
**Severability:** Invalid provisions modified; remainder continues.
**Relationship:** Independent contractors, no partnership.
