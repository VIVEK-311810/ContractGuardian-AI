## 1. SERVICES FRAMEWORK

This Master Services Agreement ("MSA") establishes the general terms under which Provider will perform IT services for Client. Specific projects defined in individual Statements of Work ("SOW") executed under this MSA.

**Service Categories:**
- Network infrastructure management
- Help desk and end-user support
- Server and cloud infrastructure management
- Cybersecurity services and monitoring
- Software deployment and updates
- Backup and disaster recovery
- IT strategy and consulting
- Project-based implementations

**Each SOW Will Specify:**
- Detailed scope and deliverables
- Timeline and milestones
- Pricing and payment terms
- Acceptance criteria
- Project-specific terms

## 2. COMPENSATION

**Rates:** As specified in each SOW. Standard rates (subject to change with 60 days notice):
- Level 1 Technician: $95/hour
- Level 2 Engineer: $135/hour
- Senior Consultant: $185/hour
- Project Manager: $150/hour

**Managed Services:** Monthly retainer pricing available for ongoing support contracts.

**Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from invoice date unless SOW specifies otherwise.

**Expenses:** Pre-approved expenses reimbursed at cost with receipts.

## 3. TERM

**Duration:** This MSA is effective March 15, 2025 and continues for 2 years.

**Renewal:** Automatically renews for 1-year terms unless either party provides 60 days written notice.

**SOW Terms:** Individual SOWs may have different durations as specified.

**Termination of MSA:**
- Either party: 60 days written notice
- For cause: 15 days to cure material breach
- MSA termination does not automatically terminate active SOWs unless specified

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**Client-Specific Work:** Deliverables created specifically for Client under SOWs become Client's property upon payment.

**Provider Tools and IP:** Provider retains all rights to pre-existing tools, methodologies, code libraries, and general knowledge.

**Third-Party Software:** Provider may use third-party software and open-source components. Client responsible for obtaining necessary licenses for software Client uses.

## 5. HIPAA AND DATA SECURITY

**Business Associate:** Provider is Client's Business Associate under HIPAA. Parties execute separate Business Associate Agreement (BAA) incorporating HIPAA requirements.

**Protected Health Information (PHI):** Provider may access PHI only as necessary to perform services. Provider implements administrative, physical, and technical safeguards per HIPAA Security Rule.

**Security Incidents:** Provider notifies Client within 24 hours of discovering security incident or PHI breach.

**Data Handling:**
- PHI encrypted at rest and in transit
- Access controls and authentication
- Audit logging of PHI access
- Secure disposal procedures

## 6. WARRANTIES

**Provider Warrants:**
- Services performed professionally consistent with industry standards
- Personnel qualified and appropriately trained
- Compliance with applicable laws and regulations
- Implementation of reasonable security measures

**Warranty Period:** 90 days from completion for defects in Provider's work

**Remedy:** Re-performance of defective services at no additional cost

**DISCLAIMER:** EXCEPT AS STATED, SERVICES PROVIDED "AS IS" WITHOUT WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 7. LIABILITY

**Liability Cap:** Provider's total aggregate liability under this MSA and all SOWs shall not exceed the greater of: (a) $250,000, or (b) total fees paid by Client in the 12 months preceding the claim.

**Excluded Damages:** Provider not liable for indirect, consequential, incidental, special, or punitive damages, lost profits, data loss, or business interruption.

**Exceptions:** Liability limitations do not apply to:
- HIPAA violations caused by Provider's gross negligence
- Breaches of confidentiality
- Provider's willful misconduct
- Death or bodily injury caused by Provider's negligence

**HIPAA Penalties:** Provider responsible for regulatory fines resulting from Provider's HIPAA violations. Client responsible for fines resulting from Client's violations.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

**Mutual Indemnification:** Each party indemnifies the other for third-party claims arising from:
- Breach of this MSA
- Negligence or willful misconduct
- Violations of applicable law

**Provider's Additional Indemnification:** IP infringement by Provider's work product (capped at liability limit)

**Client's Additional Indemnification:** Claims arising from Client's data, systems, or business operations

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties protect confidential information for 3 years after disclosure. Includes business information, technical data, PHI, pricing, and proprietary methods.

**HIPAA Information:** PHI confidentiality governed by BAA and HIPAA regulations (no expiration).

## 10. SERVICE LEVELS

**Response Times (if applicable to SOW):**
- Critical (systems down): 1 hour
- High priority: 4 hours
- Medium priority: Next business day
- Low priority: 3 business days

**Availability:** Provider provides support during business hours (8am-6pm MT, Monday-Friday) unless SOW specifies 24/7 support.

**Service Credits:** If Provider fails to meet SLA metrics in SOW, Client may receive service credits as specified in that SOW.

## 11. CHANGE MANAGEMENT

**Changes to SOWs:** Require written change order signed by both parties specifying scope changes, timeline impacts, and pricing adjustments.

**Emergency Changes:** Provider may implement emergency security patches or fixes with verbal approval, documented within 24 hours.

## 12. PERSONNEL

**Key Personnel:** Provider assigns qualified personnel. Provider may replace personnel with Client's consent (not unreasonably withheld).

**Background Checks:** Provider ensures personnel working in healthcare facilities pass background checks per Client's policies.

**Training:** Provider personnel trained on HIPAA requirements before accessing PHI.

## 13. SUBCONTRACTORS

Provider may use subcontractors with Client's prior written approval. Provider remains responsible for subcontractor performance. Subcontractors must execute confidentiality and HIPAA agreements.

## 14. INSURANCE

Provider maintains:
- Professional Liability/E&O: $2,000,000
- General Liability: $1,000,000
- Cyber Liability: $3,000,000
- Workers Compensation: Statutory

Certificates provided upon request, with Client named as additional insured.

## 15. COMPLIANCE

**Regulatory Compliance:** Both parties comply with applicable healthcare regulations, including HIPAA, HITECH, state privacy laws, and FDA regulations if applicable.

**Audits:** Client may audit Provider's HIPAA compliance annually with 30 days notice. Provider cooperates and remediates findings.

## 16. TERMINATION AND TRANSITION

**Upon Termination:**
- Provider provides reasonable transition assistance (up to 30 days at standard rates)
- Provider returns or destroys all Client data and PHI
- Provider provides documentation and knowledge transfer
- Active SOWs may continue or terminate per SOW terms

## 17. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**Governing Law:** Arizona law

**Escalation:** Disputes escalated to senior management for resolution before litigation.

**Litigation:** If unresolved, disputes litigated in Phoenix, Arizona courts. Each party bears own costs unless court awards fees.

## 18. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Entire Agreement:** This MSA and executed SOWs constitute entire agreement for services.
**Amendments:** Written amendments signed by both parties.
**Assignment:** Requires consent except to successor entity.
**Force Majeure:** Excused performance for events beyond reasonable control.
**Notices:** Via email and certified mail to addresses above.
**Severability:** Invalid provisions modified; remainder continues.
**Relationship:** Independent contractors.
**Order of Precedence:** If conflict, SOW terms prevail over MSA.
