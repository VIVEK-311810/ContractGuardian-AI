## 1. POSITION AND DUTIES

**Title:** Senior Software Engineer

**Department:** Engineering - Backend Platform Team

**Reporting To:** VP of Engineering

**Duties:** Employee shall perform software development duties including:
- Design, develop, test, and deploy backend services and APIs
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams
- Code reviews and mentoring junior engineers
- Participate in technical planning and architecture decisions
- On-call rotation for production support (approximately 1 week per month)
- Other duties as reasonably assigned

**Location:** Hybrid - Austin office 3 days/week, remote 2 days/week

**Full-Time Employment:** Employee devotes full business time, attention, and efforts to Company business.

## 2. COMPENSATION

**Base Salary:** $145,000 per year, paid bi-weekly

**Annual Bonus:** Eligible for performance bonus up to 15% of base salary, paid annually based on Company performance and individual goals (discretionary, not guaranteed)

**Equity:** 10,000 stock options with 4-year vesting schedule (25% after 1 year, then monthly vesting). Strike price determined by Board. Subject to Stock Option Agreement.

**Sign-On Bonus:** $15,000 paid with first paycheck (must repay pro-rata if leave within 12 months)

**Salary Reviews:** Annual reviews; increases at Company's discretion

## 3. BENEFITS

**Standard Benefits Package:**
- Health insurance (medical, dental, vision) - Company pays 80% of premium
- 401(k) retirement plan with 4% Company match
- 15 days paid time off (PTO) annually, accruing monthly
- 10 paid holidays
- 5 days sick leave annually
- Parental leave: 12 weeks paid
- Life insurance: 1x base salary
- Short and long-term disability insurance
- Professional development budget: $2,000/year
- Home office stipend: $1,000 annually for remote work equipment

**Benefits Subject To:** Company policies and plan documents, which may change

## 4. EMPLOYMENT TERM

**At-Will Employment:** Employment is at-will. Either party may terminate at any time, with or without cause or notice, subject to notice provisions below.

**Notice Period:**
- Employee resignation: 4 weeks written notice requested (not required)
- Company termination: 2 weeks notice or pay in lieu (except for cause)

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND PROPRIETARY INFORMATION

**Confidential Information:** Employee shall not disclose Company's confidential or proprietary information during or after employment, including:
- Source code, algorithms, and technical architecture
- Business strategies, financial data, and customer information
- Product roadmaps and unreleased features
- Employee and salary information

**Duration:** Confidentiality obligations survive employment termination indefinitely

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT

**Work Product:** All inventions, works, developments, and improvements created by Employee during employment, whether during work hours or not, that:
(a) Relate to Company's business or research
(b) Result from work performed for Company
(c) Use Company equipment, supplies, or confidential information

shall be Company's exclusive property.

**Assignment:** Employee assigns all rights, title, and interest to Company.

**Prior Inventions:** Employee may list any prior inventions to be excluded (Exhibit A). Anything not listed is presumed to be Company property if created during employment.

**Patent and Copyright Cooperation:** Employee will assist Company in obtaining patents, copyrights, and other protections at Company's expense.

## 7. NON-COMPETITION

**During Employment:** Employee shall not engage in any competing business or employment.

**Post-Employment:** For 12 months after employment ends, Employee shall not:
- Work for or provide services to any Company competitor
- Solicit Company customers or prospective customers
- Encourage Company employees to leave employment

**Geographic Scope:** United States

**Competitor Definition:** Companies that provide competing software platforms for [specific market/technology]

**Reasonableness:** Employee acknowledges this restriction is reasonable given Employee's access to confidential information and customer relationships.

## 8. NON-SOLICITATION

For 18 months after employment ends, Employee shall not:
- Solicit, recruit, or hire any Company employees or contractors
- Induce any employee to terminate employment with Company
- Interfere with Company's relationships with employees, customers, suppliers, or partners

## 9. OUTSIDE ACTIVITIES

**Approval Required:** Employee must obtain written approval for:
- Outside employment or consulting
- Service on boards of directors (except non-profits)
- Investments in competing businesses (>1% ownership)

**Permitted:** Employee may make passive investments, participate in professional associations, and engage in charitable activities that don't interfere with duties.

## 10. TERMINATION

**Termination by Company:**
- **For Cause:** Immediate termination without notice or severance for:
  - Material breach of this Agreement
  - Misconduct, dishonesty, or policy violations
  - Unsatisfactory performance after warnings
  - Conviction of felony or crime of moral turpitude
  - Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information

- **Without Cause:** Termination with 2 weeks notice or pay in lieu

**Termination by Employee:** 4 weeks notice requested to facilitate transition

**Upon Termination:**
- Final paycheck includes earned salary and accrued PTO
- Unvested stock options forfeited
- Return all Company property within 5 business days
- Confidentiality and non-compete obligations continue
- COBRA benefits information provided

**Severance (if terminated without cause):**
- 4 weeks base salary
- Continued health insurance for 1 month
- Conditional on signing separation agreement and release

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**Governing Law:** Texas law

**Arbitration:** Employment disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Austin, TX under AAA Employment Rules. Each party bears own costs. Arbitrator may award prevailing party attorney fees.

**Exceptions:** Company may seek injunctive relief in court for breaches of confidentiality, IP assignment, or non-compete provisions.

## 12. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Entire Agreement:** Supersedes all prior understandings regarding employment.

**Amendments:** Must be in writing signed by Company officer.

**Assignment:** Employee may not assign. Company may assign to successor.

**Severability:** Invalid provisions modified to be enforceable; remainder continues.

**Survival:** Confidentiality, IP assignment, non-compete, and non-solicitation survive termination.

**Background Check:** Employment conditioned on satisfactory background check.

**Immigration:** Employee authorized to work in United States and will provide I-9 documentation.

**Company Policies:** Employee shall comply with all Company policies (handbook, code of conduct, security policies, etc.).
