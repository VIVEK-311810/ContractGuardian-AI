## 1. SAAS SERVICES

Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the CloudOps Platform ("Service") for Customer's internal business operations.

**Service Includes:**
- Cloud infrastructure monitoring and management
- Automated deployment and scaling
- Security monitoring and alerts
- Performance analytics and reporting
- API access (rate limited to 10,000 calls/day)
- 24/7 technical support
- Data backup and disaster recovery
- Compliance reporting tools

**Service Level:** Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain 99.5% uptime, calculated monthly. Scheduled maintenance excluded.

**Payment Terms:**

- If monthly: First month due upon signing, subsequent months due on 1st of month

**Price Increases:** Provider may increase fees by up to 10% annually upon 60 days notice.
