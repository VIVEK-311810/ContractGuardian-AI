## 1. SCOPE

This Master Purchase Agreement ("MPA") governs Buyer's purchases of software licenses, subscriptions, and professional services from Seller. Individual purchases made via Purchase Orders ("POs") referencing this MPA.

**Products and Services:**
- Software licenses (perpetual and subscription)
- Cloud service subscriptions
- Professional services (implementation, training, consulting)
- Technical support and maintenance
- Custom development services

## 2. PURCHASE ORDERS

**PO Process:** Buyer issues POs specifying products, quantities, pricing, and delivery dates. POs incorporate this MPA's terms.

**Acceptance:** Seller accepts by acknowledging PO or commencing performance. Seller may reject within 5 business days.

**Conflicts:** If PO and MPA conflict, MPA terms prevail unless expressly modified in writing.

## 3. PRICING AND PAYMENT

**Pricing:** Per then-current price list or negotiated rates. Prices held for 90 days from quote.

**Volume Discounts:** Available per pricing schedule.

**Payment Terms:** Net 45 days from invoice. Wire transfer or ACH.

**Late Payment:** 1.5% monthly interest (18% APR) + suspension of services/licenses.

**Taxes:** Buyer pays all sales, use, and excise taxes (excluding Seller's income taxes).

## 4. DELIVERY AND ACCEPTANCE

**Delivery:** Electronic delivery for software. Services performed per SOW.

**Acceptance Period:** 15 business days to test and accept or reject with specific deficiencies. Deemed accepted if no response.

**Rejection:** Seller corrects deficiencies or provides refund for rejected items.

## 5. SOFTWARE LICENSES

**Perpetual Licenses:** One-time fee grants perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable license for internal use.

**Subscription Licenses:** Annual or multi-year terms. Auto-renew unless 60 days notice.

**Scope:** Licensed for number of users, devices, or processors specified in PO.

**Restrictions:** No reverse engineering, modification, sublicensing, or rental. No use for service bureau.

## 6. MAINTENANCE AND SUPPORT

**Maintenance:** Annual fee (20% of license cost) covers updates, bug fixes, and standard support.

**Support Levels:**
- Standard: Business hours, email/phone, 24-hour response
- Premium: 24/7, 4-hour critical response, dedicated support engineer

**Exclusions:** Issues from unauthorized modifications, third-party software, or user error.

## 7. PROFESSIONAL SERVICES

**SOW Required:** Services performed per Statements of Work specifying scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees.

**Rates:** Per rate sheet, typically $150-250/hour depending on role.

**Expenses:** Pre-approved travel and expenses billed at cost.

**Work Product:** Deliverables become Buyer's property upon payment.

## 8. WARRANTIES

**Software Warranties:**
- Performs substantially per documentation for 90 days
- Media free from defects
- No known malware

**Services Warranties:**
- Performed professionally per industry standards
- Personnel qualified and competent

**Remedy:** Repair, replace, or refund at Seller's option.

**DISCLAIMER:** EXCEPT AS STATED, PRODUCTS AND SERVICES PROVIDED "AS IS" WITHOUT WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR PURPOSE, OR NON-INFRINGEMENT.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**Liability Cap:** Seller's aggregate liability shall not exceed fees paid for the specific product/service giving rise to claim in prior 12 months.

**Excluded Damages:** No liability for indirect, consequential, incidental, special, or punitive damages, lost profits, or data loss.

**Exceptions:** Caps don't apply to payment obligations, IP infringement, or confidentiality breaches.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

**Seller Indemnifies:** IP infringement by products (subject to control of defense and liability cap).

**Buyer Indemnifies:** Claims from Buyer's data, use beyond license scope, or modifications.

## 11. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties protect confidential information for 3 years. Includes technical information, pricing, business terms.

## 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

**Term:** 3 years, auto-renews annually unless 90 days notice.

**Termination for Cause:** Material breach uncured within 30 days.

**Effect:** MPA termination doesn't affect perpetual licenses or active subscriptions/SOWs. Subscriptions and services may continue per their terms.

## 13. GENERAL

**Governing Law:** Washington State
**Dispute Resolution:** Mediation then litigation in Seattle. Prevailing party recovers fees.
**Assignment:** Requires consent except to successor.
**Force Majeure:** Excuses performance for uncontrollable events.
**Entire Agreement:** Supersedes prior agreements.
**Amendments:** Written, signed modifications only.
**Severability:** Invalid terms modified; remainder continues.
