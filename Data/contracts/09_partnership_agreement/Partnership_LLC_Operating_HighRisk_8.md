## 1. FORMATION AND PURPOSE

**Purpose:** Develop and commercialize data analytics software for enterprise clients.

**Term:** Perpetual until dissolved per this Agreement.

**Principal Office:** 1200 Market Street, Suite 400, San Francisco, CA 94102

## 2. CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

**Initial:** Each Member contributed $150,000.

**Additional Contributions:** May be required by unanimous vote. Members contribute pro-rata to ownership. Failure to contribute results in dilution.

**Capital Accounts:** Maintained per IRS regulations.

**No Interest:** Capital contributions do not earn interest.

## 3. OWNERSHIP AND DISTRIBUTIONS

**Ownership:** 50/50 split (500 units each)

**Distributions:** Quarterly distributions of available cash as determined by Members. Distributions pro-rata to ownership unless otherwise agreed.

**Priority:** Distributions first to return capital contributions, then pro-rata profits.

**Tax Distributions:** Minimum annual distributions to cover tax liabilities on allocated income.

## 4. MANAGEMENT AND VOTING

**Member-Managed:** Both Members participate in management.

**Voting:** One vote per unit (500 votes each).

**Unanimous Required For:**
- Amendments to Operating Agreement
- Admission of new members
- Sale, merger, or dissolution of Company
- Loans or guarantees over $50,000
- Real estate purchases or leases over $100,000
- Changes to business purpose
- Hiring or firing officers

**Majority Required For:**
- Day-to-day business decisions
- Contracts under $50,000
- Hiring employees
- Routine operational matters

**Deadlock:** If Members deadlock on unanimous decision, matter fails unless resolved through mediation.

## 5. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

**Alex Johnson - CEO:**
- Overall business strategy
- Fundraising and investor relations
- Sales and business development
- Final authority on business strategy (if deadlocked)

**Morgan Chen - CTO:**
- Technology development and architecture
- Engineering team management
- Product roadmap
- Final authority on technical decisions (if deadlocked)

**Compensation:** Salaries determined by unanimous vote, currently:
- CEO: $120,000/year
- CTO: $120,000/year

**Time Commitment:** Full-time devotion to Company business.

## 6. ALLOCATION OF PROFITS AND LOSSES

**Allocation:** Pro-rata to ownership (50/50)

**Tax Treatment:** LLC taxed as partnership. Each Member receives K-1.

**Special Allocations:** Per IRC regulations and capital account maintenance.

## 7. TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS

**Right of First Refusal:** Member wishing to sell must first offer to other Member at same price and terms. Other Member has 30 days to accept.

**Approval Required:** Transfer of more than 10% requires unanimous consent.

**Permitted Transfers:** To family trusts or estate planning vehicles with Member retaining voting control.

**Drag-Along:** If one Member agrees to sell Company, can force other Member to participate on same terms.

**Tag-Along:** If one Member sells stake, other Member can participate pro-rata.

## 8. BUYOUT AND VALUATION

**Triggering Events:**
- Voluntary withdrawal
- Death or disability
- Material breach of Agreement
- Deadlock lasting 90+ days

**Valuation Method:**
1. Good faith negotiation (30 days)
2. If no agreement, independent valuation by mutually agreed appraiser
3. Valuation based on 12-month trailing revenue × industry multiple, adjusted for assets/liabilities

**Payment Terms:** 20% cash at closing, remainder over 36 months with 6% interest.

**Buy-Sell (Shotgun Clause):** Either Member may offer to buy other's interest at specified price. Receiving Member must either sell at that price or buy offering Member's interest at same price within 30 days.

## 9. DEATH OR DISABILITY

**Death:** Deceased Member's estate must sell interest to surviving Member at fair market value per Section 8 valuation.

**Disability:** If Member unable to perform duties for 6+ months, other Member may purchase interest at fair market value.

**Life Insurance:** Members encouraged to obtain life insurance policies to fund buyout.

## 10. COMPETE AND CONFIDENTIALITY

**Non-Compete:** During membership and for 24 months after separation, Members shall not:
- Start competing data analytics business
- Work for or invest in direct competitors
- Solicit Company customers or employees

**Confidentiality:** Perpetual obligation to protect Company confidential information.

**IP Assignment:** All work product and inventions created during membership belong to Company.

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**Mediation:** Disputes first submitted to mediation in San Francisco within 30 days.

**Arbitration:** If mediation fails, binding arbitration under AAA rules. Each party bears own costs.

**Governing Law:** Delaware LLC law; disputes resolved in Delaware or California.

**Injunctive Relief:** Either Member may seek injunction for breaches of non-compete or confidentiality.

## 12. DISSOLUTION

**Events:**
- Unanimous vote
- Bankruptcy of Member
- Judicial dissolution
- Purpose accomplished or impossible

**Winding Up:** Remaining Member may wind up or continue with new member.

**Distribution Priority:**
1. Creditors and obligations
2. Return of capital contributions
3. Remaining assets per ownership

## 13. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS

**Books and Records:** Maintained at principal office. Members have inspection rights.

**Fiscal Year:** Calendar year.

**Bank Accounts:** Signatures of either Member required.

**Annual Meetings:** Quarterly business reviews.

**Amendments:** Require unanimous written consent.

**Severability:** Invalid provisions modified; remainder continues.

**Entire Agreement:** Supersedes all prior agreements.
