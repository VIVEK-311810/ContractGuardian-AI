## 1. PURPOSE

The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship related to artificial intelligence and quantum computing integration ("Purpose"). Each party may disclose proprietary and confidential information to the other.

## 2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" includes all technical, business, financial, and other information disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally, in writing, or electronically, including:
- Technical data, algorithms, source code, and specifications
- Business plans, strategies, and financial information
- Customer lists, pricing, and marketing plans
- Research and development information
- Product roadmaps and future plans

## 3. OBLIGATIONS

Each Receiving Party agrees to:
- Maintain Confidential Information in strict confidence
- Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose
- Not disclose to third parties without prior written consent
- Protect using same degree of care as own confidential information (minimum reasonable care)
- Limit access to employees, contractors, and advisors with need-to-know who are bound by confidentiality
- Not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any disclosed technology

## 4. EXCLUSIONS

Information is not confidential if it:
- Was publicly known at time of disclosure
- Becomes public through no fault of Receiving Party
- Was rightfully possessed prior to disclosure
- Is independently developed without use of Confidential Information
- Is disclosed with prior written approval
- Must be disclosed by law or court order (with prompt notice)

## 5. TERM

This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and continues for 3 years. Confidentiality obligations survive for 2 years after termination, except trade secrets which are protected indefinitely or until no longer qualify as trade secrets under applicable law.

## 6. RETURN OF MATERIALS

Upon request or termination, each party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify destruction in writing.

## 7. NO LICENSE OR WARRANTY

No license or rights are granted except as explicitly stated. Disclosing Party makes no warranty regarding accuracy or completeness of Confidential Information.

## 8. REMEDIES

Breach may cause irreparable harm. Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies. Each party acknowledges monetary damages may be inadequate.

## 9. NO OBLIGATION

This Agreement does not obligate either party to enter any business relationship, disclose information, or proceed with the Purpose.
