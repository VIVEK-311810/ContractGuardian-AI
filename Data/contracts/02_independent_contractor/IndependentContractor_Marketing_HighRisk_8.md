## 1. SERVICES

Contractor agrees to provide comprehensive digital marketing services to Company, including:

### Marketing Deliverables:
- Development and execution of social media marketing strategies
- Content creation (blog posts, social media content, email campaigns)
- Search engine optimization (SEO) and sem management
- Google Ads and Facebook Ads campaign management
- Marketing automation setup and management (HubSpot, Marketo)
- Analytics tracking and monthly reporting
- Brand positioning and messaging development
- Lead generation campaigns
- Email marketing campaigns and newsletter management
- Influencer partnership coordination

### Specific Responsibilities:
- Manage all Company social media accounts (LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, Facebook)
- Create minimum 20 social media posts per week
- Write 4 blog posts per month (800-1200 words each)
- Design and send 8 email campaigns per month
- Manage monthly advertising budget of $15,000-25,000
- Provide monthly analytics reports with KPIs and recommendations
- Coordinate with external agencies, freelancers, and vendors
- Attend weekly strategy meetings (remote or in-person)

**Work Hours:** Contractor shall dedicate approximately 30-35 hours per week to Company's marketing needs, with flexibility for campaign launches and urgent matters.

## 2. COMPENSATION

**Monthly Fee:** $8,500 USD

**Payment Terms:**
- Invoices submitted by the 1st of each month for previous month's services
- Payment due within 20 days of invoice date
- Late payments subject to 1.5% monthly interest
- Payments made via ACH or wire transfer

**Advertising Budget:** Separate from contractor fee; Company provides corporate credit card or reimbursement for approved advertising spend.

**Expenses:** Company reimburses reasonable, pre-approved business expenses (software subscriptions, stock photos, professional development) within 30 days of submission with receipts.

## 3. TERM AND TERMINATION

**Initial Term:** 12 months commencing March 1, 2025

**Auto-Renewal:** This Agreement automatically renews for successive 12-month terms unless either party provides 60 days written notice of non-renewal prior to the end of the current term.

**Termination for Convenience:**
- Company may terminate at any time with 30 days written notice
- Contractor may terminate with 60 days written notice
- Upon termination by Company, Contractor receives payment for work performed through termination date
- Upon termination by Contractor, Contractor forfeits final month's compensation and must provide transition assistance

**Termination for Cause:**
Either party may terminate immediately for material breach if not cured within 10 days of written notice.

**Post-Termination Obligations:**
- Contractor must immediately cease using Company branding and materials
- Contractor must deliver all work product, logins, and access credentials
- Contractor must provide reasonable transition assistance (up to 20 hours at no charge)
- All restrictive covenants remain in full force

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**Work for Hire:** All materials, content, strategies, campaigns, and creative work developed by Contractor in connection with services hereunder are "works made for hire" and shall be the exclusive property of Company. This includes:
- Blog posts, articles, and written content
- Social media posts and creative assets
- Email templates and campaign materials
- Marketing strategies and campaign plans
- Analytics reports and presentations
- Brand messaging and positioning documents
- Advertising creative and ad copy

**Assignment:** To the extent any work does not qualify as work for hire, Contractor hereby assigns all rights, title, and interest to Company.

**Contractor Tools:** Contractor retains rights to pre-existing templates, frameworks, and methodologies. Company receives a non-exclusive license to use these materials as incorporated in deliverables.

**Third-Party Content:** Contractor represents that all content uses properly licensed images, fonts, and materials. Contractor is responsible for securing necessary licenses for stock photos, music, or other third-party content.

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE

**Confidential Information:** Contractor acknowledges access to Company's confidential and proprietary information including business strategies, customer lists, pricing, financial data, product roadmaps, marketing plans, and trade secrets.

**Obligations:** Contractor agrees to:
- Maintain strict confidentiality indefinitely (no expiration date)
- Use confidential information solely for performing services
- Not disclose to any third party without prior written consent
- Return or destroy all confidential information upon termination
- Implement reasonable security measures to protect information

**Breach Consequences:** Breach of confidentiality may result in immediate termination and legal action for damages and injunctive relief.

## 6. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION

**Non-Compete:** During the term of this Agreement and for 24 months following termination, Contractor shall not:
- Provide marketing services to any Company competitor
- Work for or consult with companies in the enterprise software industry
- Engage in any business that competes with Company's products or services
- Market or promote competitive products or services

**Geographic Scope:** United States and Canada

**Definition of Competitor:** Any company that offers enterprise software solutions for project management, team collaboration, or workflow automation (as determined by Company in its sole discretion).

**Non-Solicitation:** For 24 months after termination, Contractor shall not:
- Solicit, recruit, or hire any Company employees or contractors
- Encourage any Company employees to leave their employment
- Solicit or do business with any Company clients or prospective clients Contractor interacted with during the engagement
- Interfere with Company's relationships with suppliers, partners, or vendors

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

**Contractor's Indemnification:** Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Company, its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from:
- Contractor's breach of this Agreement
- Contractor's negligence or willful misconduct
- Copyright, trademark, or other IP infringement claims related to content created by Contractor
- False or misleading advertising claims
- Violations of FTC regulations or advertising laws
- Data privacy violations (GDPR, CCPA, CAN-SPAM)

**No Limitation:** This indemnification obligation has no monetary cap and survives termination indefinitely.

Company's total liability to Contractor under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid to Contractor in the 3 months preceding the claim.

Contractor acknowledges that marketing results depend on numerous factors beyond Company's control and agrees that Company is not liable for:
- Failure to achieve specific marketing metrics or business results
- Changes to social media platform algorithms or policies
- Advertising account suspensions or restrictions
- Search engine algorithm updates affecting rankings

## 10. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

**Relationship:** Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or partner of Company.

**Taxes and Benefits:** Contractor is responsible for:

- Self-employment taxes
- Required business licenses and permits

- General liability insurance (minimum $1,000,000 coverage)

**Insurance:** Contractor must maintain required insurance throughout the engagement and provide certificates of insurance upon request.

**Control:** Contractor controls the means and methods of work, subject to Company's right to specify deliverables, deadlines, and quality standards.

## 11. PERFORMANCE STANDARDS

**Quality Standards:** All work must meet Company's quality standards and brand guidelines. Company may reject work that does not meet standards and require revisions at no additional cost.

**Metrics and KPIs:** Contractor's performance will be evaluated based on:
- Social media engagement rates
- Website traffic and lead generation
- Email campaign performance (open rates, click rates)
- Advertising ROAS (return on ad spend)
- Content quality and brand consistency
- Timely delivery of deliverables

**Performance Reviews:** Quarterly reviews to assess performance and adjust strategies.
