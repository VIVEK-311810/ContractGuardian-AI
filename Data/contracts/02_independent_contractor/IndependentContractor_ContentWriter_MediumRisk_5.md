## 1. SERVICES

Contractor shall provide professional content writing services including:
- Blog articles (4-6 per month, 1000-1500 words each)
- Website copy and landing pages
- White papers and case studies (1-2 per quarter)
- Email newsletters and marketing copy
- SEO-optimized content with keyword integration
- Content editing and proofreading
- Content calendar planning and management

**Deliverables:** All content must be original, well-researched, grammatically correct, and aligned with Company's brand voice and style guidelines.

## 2. COMPENSATION

**Rate Structure:**
- Blog articles: $250 per article (1000-1500 words)
- Website copy: $150 per page
- White papers: $800-1200 per paper (2500-4000 words)
- Email content: $100 per email
- Editing services: $75/hour

**Payment Terms:**
- Invoices submitted bi-weekly for completed and approved work
- Payment due within 15 days via ACH transfer
- Revisions beyond 2 rounds billed at $75/hour

**Estimated Monthly Value:** $2,500-3,500 depending on workload

## 3. TERM

**Duration:** This is an ongoing agreement with no fixed end date.

**Termination:** Either party may terminate with 14 days written notice. Upon termination:
- Contractor completes work in progress or Company pays pro-rated amount
- Company pays for all completed and approved work
- Contractor delivers all drafts and materials

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**Work for Hire:** All content created is Company's exclusive property upon payment. Contractor may include work samples in portfolio with Company permission.

**Originality:** Contractor warrants all content is original, not plagiarized, and doesn't infringe third-party rights.

## 5. REVISIONS AND APPROVAL

Company may request up to 2 rounds of revisions at no charge. Additional revisions billed at $75/hour. Company must provide feedback within 5 business days or content is deemed approved.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Contractor agrees to maintain confidentiality of Company information for 2 years after termination. Includes business plans, unpublished content, and strategic information.

## 7. NON-COMPETE

For 6 months after termination, Contractor will not create content for direct competitors of Company as mutually identified in writing.

## 8. WARRANTIES

Contractor warrants professional work quality, timely delivery, and compliance with Company guidelines. Company responsible for factual accuracy of information provided to Contractor.

## 9. LIABILITY

**Contractor's Liability:** Capped at fees paid in previous 3 months. Not liable for indirect or consequential damages.

**Indemnification:** Contractor indemnifies Company (up to liability cap) for plagiarism or IP infringement claims. Company indemnifies Contractor for claims arising from Company-provided information or instructions.

## 10. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

Contractor is not an employee. Contractor responsible for own taxes, insurance, and business expenses. No benefits provided.

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**Governing Law:** California law

**Disputes:** Good-faith negotiation, then mediation, then arbitration in San Francisco if needed. Each party bears own costs.

## 12. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Entire Agreement:** Supersedes prior agreements.
**Amendments:** Written amendments signed by both parties.
**Assignment:** Requires written consent.
**Severability:** Invalid provisions modified; remainder continues.
